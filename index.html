01 / THE INITIATIVE

Building the foundation
for deep-space ambition.

AdiShakti Aerospace is an emerging aerospace initiative focused on research, engineering concepts, technology development and long-horizon exploration.

Our flagship vision, SHAKTIYAAN-U1, is a conceptual Uranus orbiter and atmospheric probe mission. The goal is to turn an ambitious idea into a disciplined engineering roadmap — one technology step at a time.

CONCEPT STATUS • This website describes a concept and future development vision, not an operational spacecraft or completed launch program.

Uranus Orbiter + Atmospheric Probe

03 / MISSION JOURNEY

From Earth
to Uranus.

01

Launch & Earth Orbit

Conceptual launch vehicle places the spacecraft into an initial Earth orbit.

02

Trans-Uranus Injection

Departure from Earth on a carefully designed interplanetary trajectory.

03

Deep-Space Cruise

Long-duration cruise with navigation, communications and trajectory corrections.

04

Uranus Arrival

Orbital insertion concept followed by spacecraft commissioning and science operations.

05

Probe Deployment

An atmospheric probe concept descends into Uranus while the orbiter relays data.

06

Science & Data Relay

Measurements and images are stored, relayed to Earth and analyzed by scientists.
